# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

# Executive Financial Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Executive Financial Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed in Microsoft Power BI using the Microsoft Financial Sample dataset.

The purpose of the dashboard is to transform financial data into meaningful insights that support strategic business decisions through interactive visualizations, KPI monitoring, and executive reporting.

---

# Business Problem

Business leaders require a centralized dashboard to monitor financial performance, evaluate product profitability, identify regional trends, and assess the impact of pricing and discount strategies.

Without a centralized reporting solution, management faces challenges in:

- Monitoring overall business performance
- Identifying profitable products
- Comparing regional performance
- Evaluating financial trends
- Making timely data-driven decisions

---

# Project Objectives

The dashboard aims to:

- Monitor overall financial performance
- Track key financial KPIs
- Analyze product performance
- Evaluate regional sales and profitability
- Assess discount effectiveness
- Support executive decision-making
- Deliver actionable business insights
