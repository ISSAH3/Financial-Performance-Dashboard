# 📌 Project Overview

The **Executive Financial Analytics Dashboard** is a professional Business Intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI** and the **Microsoft Financial Sample Dataset**.

The dashboard transforms raw financial data into interactive visualizations and executive-level insights to support strategic decision-making.

This project demonstrates practical experience in:

- Financial Analytics
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- Power Query Data Transformation
- Data Modeling

# 🎯 Business Problem

Business leaders need a centralized reporting solution to monitor financial performance, evaluate profitability, identify regional trends, and make informed strategic decisions.

Without interactive reporting, decision-makers may struggle to:

- Monitor KPIs efficiently
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze product profitability
- Evaluate pricing and discount strategies
- Identify growth opportunities

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

This dashboard was developed to:

- Monitor financial performance
- Analyze product profitability
- Evaluate regional performance
- Assess discount effectiveness
- Support executive decision-making
- Present actionable business insights

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 📍 Page 1 – Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance.

Features:

- Executive KPI Cards
- Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Country
- Top Products
- Financial Overview

---

## 📦 Page 2 – Product Performance Analytics

Focuses on product-level business performance.

Features:

- Product KPIs
- Product Revenue
- Product Profit
- Product Contribution
- Product Ranking

---

## 🌍 Page 3 – Regional Performance Analytics

Compares countries and regional financial performance.

Features:

- Regional KPIs
- Revenue by Country
- Profit by Country
- Regional Rankings
- Geographic Analysis

---

## 📈 Page 4 – Sales & Financial Intelligence

Analyzes financial performance and discount effectiveness.

Features:

- Gross Sales vs Net Sales
- Profit Trend
- Discount Analysis
- Financial KPIs

---

## 📋 Page 5 – Executive Summary

Summarizes key findings and business recommendations.

Includes:

- Executive Insights
- Business Risks
- Opportunities
- Strategic Recommendations
- Management Action Plan

# 📈 Key Business Insights

From the dashboard analysis:

- Revenue remained consistent throughout the reporting period.
- A small number of products generated the majority of total profit.
- Regional performance varied across countries.
- Discount strategies influenced overall profitability.
- Profitability remained positive despite discounts.
- Interactive KPIs enabled executive monitoring of financial performance.
